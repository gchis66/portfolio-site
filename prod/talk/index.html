You are talking to an AI that represents me. It answers from a curated knowledge base and will say so when it does not know something. Nothing is recorded or stored. The transcript stays in your browser and I never receive a copy. Sessions are capped at 5 minutes. Please do not share anything sensitive.

What you will be able to ask it

What is your AWS experience? Tell me about a project that failed. Are you open to contract work? What are you looking for in a role? Walk me through how this voice agent works.

Why this is not just a chatbot

An agent that speaks on my behalf cannot afford to be creative with facts. If it invents a certification I do not hold or a job I never had, that is not a cute hallucination, it is a lie with my name on it.

So every factual claim it makes has to be traceable to a source in the knowledge base, and when there is no source it is required to say "I would have to check with Greg" rather than improvise. Every transcript is audited afterward for claims that cannot be matched to a source.

Enforcing that, and proving it holds, is the actual engineering here. The voice part is the easy half.

How it is built

Realtime speech-to-speech over WebRTC, retrieval-grounded responses, a small tool allowlist, and hard cost ceilings, deployed on AWS through the same OIDC pipeline as the rest of this site.
